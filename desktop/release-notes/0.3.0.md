Desktop installers for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux. Unsigned for now: see desktop/README.md for opening them.

HandShake is now open source, under the Cryptographic Autonomy License 1.0.

## For customers

- **A cart for each store**, kept as you left it, and checked against the store as it is now: a new price is shown beside the old one, an item that's gone or sold out is marked to remove, and choices that no longer fit are chosen again. Nothing is ordered until each is dealt with.
- **Free delivery on bigger orders**, where a store offers it, with how much more reaches it shown in your cart.
- **Deals for delivery or for pickup**, with a switch to the way a deal needs.
- **See who's bringing your order**: their first name, photo and car, on a map that follows the roads.
- **Report missing or wrong items, or a poor delivery**: the store refunds all, part or none, and may add points as an apology, shown on your order.
- **Your own record**, as stores and drivers see it, and **blocking** any store or driver.
- **Order again** from the home screen and from any finished order.
- **Appearance**: follow your device, or always light or dark. Less motion when your device asks for it.

## For stores

- **Cover more of delivery on bigger orders**, up to free delivery, and make any deal or promo code for delivery or pickup only.
- **Refund all, part or none** of a customer's report, and give points as an apology or a thank-you on any finished order.
- **Rate customers**, see their report history, and block those you won't serve.
- **Take things down**: menu items, choice groups, goodie bags ended early. Rewards whose item has left the menu are flagged.
- **A tax report** of what you sold, and a store's issues in one place.

## For drivers

- **Set your own delivery fee**: a rate per mile or km, or up to ten distance bands.
- **New orders near you**, wherever you are in the app, with offers from stores near the one you're collecting from.

## Under the hood

- Card payments hold exactly once through processor outages, crashes and restarts; processor keys are entered in a window of the desktop app's own.
- Recovery takes a majority of guardians, and moving to a new phone checks its handover before anything is written.
- A read that fails is never taken as "nothing there", in payments, rewards and recovery.
